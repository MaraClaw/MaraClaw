# app/services

This is the main business layer. It is intentionally mixed: flat service modules plus specialized runtime subpackages.

## Boundaries

- Keep route handlers thin. Reusable domain behavior belongs in services.
- New storage behavior goes under `storage_runtime/`.
- New sandbox backends go under `sandbox/local`, `sandbox/api`, or `sandbox/remote` and are registered through `sandbox/registry.py`. Read `sandbox/AGENTS.md` and `sandbox/local/AGENTS.md` before changing isolation, proxy, or bwrap behavior.
- New trigger behavior goes under `trigger_runtime/`, not in `trigger_daemon.py` unless it is process-loop wiring.
- New LLM provider/protocol work goes under `llm/`, in `base.py`, `types.py`, `registry.py`, `factory.py`, or `providers/`. The `llm/client.py` facade is retired; import from the owning module. Company pool RBAC (who may add keys / assign tenant models) lives in `enterprise_llm.py`; routes stay thin. SuperGrok / X Premium device-code is `grok_oauth.py` (fakeable HTTP) + `grok_subscription.py` (session + encrypted pool upsert + refresh). ChatGPT Plus / Pro / Team uses browser OAuth PKCE with an engine-hosted callback (`app/api/chatgpt_oauth_callback.py`): `chatgpt_pkce.py` builds the authorize URL from `CHATGPT_OAUTH_CLIENT_ID` / `CHATGPT_OAUTH_REDIRECT_URI` and strictly parses the redirect query, `chatgpt_subscription_store.py` owns encrypted-verifier Redis tickets and their SHA-256 state index (600s, GETDEL claim, owner-bound 300s results), `chatgpt_subscription_handoff.py` owns short transaction/network boundaries and, because the callback has no session, re-authorizes the recorded owner (`subscription_boundaries.fresh_subscription_owner`), `chatgpt_oauth.py` exchanges/refreshes tokens, and `chatgpt_subscription.py` persists encrypted credentials and activates pool rows. No ChatGPT device fallback or process-local pending state. Engine inference builds clients with `create_fresh_llm_client_from_model` (refreshes a stale subscription token, then Codex Responses for ChatGPT), not `/chat/completions`; the direct `create_llm_client_from_model` constructor never refreshes. Details: `llm/AGENTS.md`.
- Per-account agent caps: `quota_guard.agent_limit` / `agent_capacity` are the only policy (tier cap, `OrganizationAgentLimit` via the plan catalog, from `tenants.tier`; only an account with no organization keeps the stored user quota). Creation (`agent_provisioning`), expired-agent renewal (`check_agent_renewal_quota`), ownership transfer and paid company transfer reuse it under the shared tenant/user locks; renewal counts against the owner, not the acting admin. Do not add route-level policy checks. See `../../docs/organization-pricing.md`.
- Company-wide caps: `company_capacity.py` is the only policy for a non-system organization (`company_caps`: plan `included_accounts` / `included_agents` plus `capacity_ledger.effective_extras`; system organizations are uncapped, `is_system` first, and `company_caps` is `None` for nothing else). Gates `require_account_slot` / `require_agent_slots` lock the tenant row, then count; membership writers (`org_membership._set_user_org`, `org_membership.activate_verified_memberships` for email verification and SMTP-less login, `registration_service.create_user_with_identity`, `admin_provisioning` create/activate, `tenant_provisioning.attach_genesis_org_admin`), company re-enable (`tenant_lifecycle` restores only `free_account_slots`, org admins first, through `UserDAO.reactivate_for_tenant(limit=...)`), the invite endpoints (advisory), `check_agent_creation_quota` (creation and renewal) and the paid transfer destination call them. `CompanyAccountLimitReachedError` maps to 409 in `app/main.py`. Do not add route-level policy checks. See `../../docs/organization-pricing.md`.
- Extra accounts and agents (paid): `capacity_orders.py` is the only order flow (quote, evidence, cancel, platform-admin review; every transition an `UPDATE` guarded by the expected status under the order row lock, decisions and creation also under the tenant row lock). `capacity_pricing.py` is the pure exact arithmetic (`Fraction`, proration on the Vietnam calendar, USD cents half up, VND up to the next thousand; never a float). `capacity_ledger.py` turns approved orders into `effective_extras` (SQL in the module; `CapacityExtras`, `vn_today`, the 7-day `grace_until` helpers). `capacity_renewals.py` generates the primary renewal of each month (tenant row lock, idempotent), supplements late approvals and expires stale orders; the `capacity_renewal` loop is `capacity_renewal_worker.py`. `capacity_payment_settings.py` owns the genesis-only bank details (`system_settings` key `capacity_payment_settings`, reserved from the generic enterprise endpoints), `capacity_evidence.py` the magic-byte sniffing, `_capacity_payments/` and `_organization_admissions/` keys and the evidence store/discard/download helpers both payment flows share, `capacity_notifications.py` the four `NotificationKind`s, `coded_errors.py` the `{code, message}` HTTP errors (`conflict`) shared by capacity orders and organization admissions. Rules and examples: `../../docs/organization-pricing.md`.
- Paid organization admission: `organization_admissions.py` owns the flow (`quote_request` prices the first period and refuses known email/domain/pending conflicts, `submit_request` verifies nothing about the client beyond the signed terms, rate-limits, stores evidence, inserts the pending row and notifies in one transaction, `read_evidence` is genesis-only). `organization_admission_quote_token.py` mints and verifies the HMAC token (key derived from `JWT_SECRET_KEY` with a fixed purpose label; expiry takes an injected `now`), `organization_admission_rate_limit.py` is the per-IP Redis fixed window (fails open with a warning), `organization_plans.first_period_price_usd` and `capacity_pricing.round_up_vnd` are the pricing rules. Only the first period is collected; there is no recurring base-fee billing. Rules: `../../docs/organization-pricing.md`.
- Per-agent daily message cap: `agent_message_quota.py` is the only policy (tier cap `OrganizationDailyAgentMessageLimit`, local day of the genesis org admin's timezone, UTC fallback). `charge_agent_daily_message` is charged right after `charge_conversation_quota` (which skips every user inside an organization) in `chat_submissions`, `channels/submissions` and `channels/personal_burst_admission` only (never greetings, schedules, triggers, heartbeats or agent-to-agent turns); admin read and reset reuse it. Counter table and atomic charge: `dao/agent_daily_message_usage_dao.py`.
- New document conversion belongs in `document_conversion/`, not in `agent_tools.py`.
- Inbound office-document parsing belongs in `document_parser/` (local `anydoc` / `firecrawl-anydoc`). Do not add format-specific extractors in `files.py` or `agent_tool_exec/document_reading.py`. `text_extractor.py` is gone — do not recreate it. Ordinary text decoding stays local. Do not route CSV/HTML/Markdown generation through anydoc.
- New OKR behavior should use focused `okr_*.py` modules. Dashboard period math is `okr_periods.py` (UTC). HTTP models are `app/schemas/okr.py`. Do not grow `okr_reporting.py`. Gap/outreach still lives in `app/api/okr.py`.
- Provider organization department/people sync and its adapter/coordinator package are retired. Shared `org_members` identity/contact/profile data and agent relationships remain; do not recreate the sync subsystem.
- Org departments belong in `org_departments.py` (multiple departments per end user). Set replacement validates every department before writing, locks via `acl_locks`, and transactionally bumps agent ACL versions for the union of old/new `scope_type=department` grants. Singular assignment delegates to replace-with-one or clear.
- New tool execution handlers belong in `agent_tool_exec/` (`@register`). Seed rows go in `tool_definitions/` (composer `builtin.py`). OpenAI catalog shapes go in `agent_tools_definitions/` (package). Runtime visibility/config in `tool_runtime/`. Keep `agent_tools.py` and `tool_seeder.py` as compatibility/orchestration surfaces. Page-read lives in `agent_tool_exec/web_read.py` (`read_webpage`). Web search/fetch are guest image-bundled Tavily skills; `tavily_runtime.py` supplies config keys and engine HTTP is `tavily/` key-ring/proxy — not a function-calling tool.
- `tool_config.py` owns tenant-scoped tool settings (`tenant_settings`) and sensitive-field encryption/decryption/masking helpers. Distinct from `tool_runtime/tool_config.py`, which loads, merges, and caches per-agent runtime config.

## God Files

- `agent_tools.py` is the primary no-more-growth file. Avoid adding unrelated tool families, conversion helpers, dispatch branches, or catalog tables there. Leftover names are `@register`ed in `agent_tool_exec/_agent_tool_exec_leftover.py`; Normal `dispatcher.execute_tool` uses the registry, with MCP execution for unregistered names, not duplicate builtin branches; the post-approval `_execute_tool_direct` is a separate fixed builtin allowlist (`agent_tool_exec/AGENTS.md`).
- Keep provider logic in the focused LLM modules, not a recreated `llm/client.py` facade. `tool_seeder.py`, `agentbay_client.py`, `agent_seeder.py`, `feishu_service.py`, `okr_reporting.py`, and `auth_provider.py` are also large enough to prefer adjacent focused modules for new work.
- Workspace folder deletes and moves (`workspace_collaboration.delete_storage_tree` / `move_storage_tree`) list once with ETags, batch through the storage batch API (`storage_runtime/AGENTS.md`), record one revision per file, and name every left-behind path in `WorkspaceWriteResult.rejected_paths`. `workspace_locks` renews its Redis locks every third of the TTL while the body runs (injectable `clock`); `check_workspace_lock()` raises `WorkspaceLockLostError` between steps once a renewal found another owner, and a Redis error (pool overload included) only counts as lost after a full TTL without a renewal.
- Workspace text that feeds the model is read in bounded heads (`storage_runtime/text_head.py`), never whole. `heartbeat._run_heartbeat` takes at most `HEARTBEAT_MD_MAX_CHARS` (16,000) characters of `HEARTBEAT.md` as the instruction and ends it in `...(truncated)` when the file is longer; the privacy and posting rules appended after it are unchanged, and a blank file keeps the default instruction. `agent_context._read_file_safe(key, max_chars)` reads `max_chars * 4 + 4` bytes and returns the stripped head cut to `max_chars` (soul 30,000, memory 2,000, default 3,000), plus the marker when the file goes on; it equals a whole read whenever the file fits. The skills index reads `SKILL_HEAD_BYTES` (64 KiB) of each `SKILL.md`, `skill.md` and flat `.md`: a frontmatter or first description line inside the head gives the same name and description, and frontmatter whose closing `---` lies beyond it is parsed like a file without one. Tests: `tests/test_agent_context.py`, `tests/test_heartbeat.py`.
- Other flat hotspots include `skill_seeder.py`, `resource_discovery.py`, `heartbeat.py`, `agent_context.py`, `workspace_collaboration.py`, `email_service.py`, `template_seeder.py`, `channel_user_service.py`, `sso_service.py`, `dingtalk_stream.py`, and `okr_scheduler.py`; extend them only when the change belongs to that exact domain.
- `chat_persist.py` wraps post-LLM message/session/`last_active_at` writes in one `connection_ctx`. `agent_context_cache.py` is the short-TTL Redis cache for soul/memory/skills on the cache client under the `redis_cache` breaker; `build_agent_context` reads all three kinds in one `read_cached_texts` round trip (a Redis outage costs a turn at most one cache timeout) and misses go through `load_cached_text`, which shares one load and fill per agent, kind and observed version. Invalidate on those workspace writes only. Sets carry `observed_ver` so a stale fill cannot win after invalidate.
- `im_token_cache.py` shares Feishu/WeCom/DingTalk/Google Chat/Teams bearer tokens across workers. Keys hash public id **and** secret; Google Chat keys the service-account email, a SHA-256 of the private key and the scopes, Teams the app id, tenant and a SHA-256 of the client secret (managed identity has no secret), so a rotated key or secret never reuses a token. DingTalk OAuth2 vs oapi use different provider prefixes. Misses go through `get_or_mint_im_token` (one task-owned mint per key per event loop; failures reach only current waiters and are never cached). Rotation drops are unconditional; provider-rejection drops use `drop_rejected_im_token` (compare-and-delete). Native-event acceptance and replay are decided by the durable `channels/submissions.py` receipts, never by a cache.
- `agent_runtime/` is removed. Do not recreate it, add `AGENTS.md`, or treat it as live. Same for deleted `group_*` / `heartbeat_runtime` bytecode.

## Guest Lifecycle

Operator policy and limitations: [guest containers](../../docs/guest-containers.md).

- Keep orchestration in `agent_lifecycle.py`; identity, admission, limits, ports, the guest image rule, readiness and observed diagnostics belong in `guest_identity.py`, `guest_admission.py`, `guest_limits.py` (tier or global limits, `limits_differ`), `guest_ports.py`, `guest_image.py` (the local-only image rule and the image check before admission), `guest_readiness.py` (bounded readiness and early exit on Docker state) and `guest_container_status.py`, not new branches in `agent_manager.py`.
- Guest Docker CLI calls (start, stop, admission, the image check, readiness, the orphan sweep and the metrics container status) go through `docker_capacity.run_docker`: a per-event-loop lane of `DOCKER_LANE_THREADS` threads apart from anyio's default limiter, one slot per call and never across a start or stop sequence. Interactive calls (user start and stop) are admitted ahead of background ones; each priority queues at most `DOCKER_LANE_QUEUE_LIMIT` calls, and a full queue raises `DockerBusyError` (503 + `Retry-After` in `app/main.py`; a start refused this way stays pending under `capacity_exceeded`, a stop changes nothing). Every sweep and worker (start reconciler, container recovery, deletion and transfer cleanup, orphan sweep, resource-pressure and workspace-quota sampling, admin runtime and tier-change observations) reaches the lane at background priority (`priority=DockerPriority.BACKGROUND` on one call, `background_docker()` around nested service calls) and treats `DockerBusyError` as a skipped pass; no Docker CLI call belongs on `to_thread`. A cancelled mutation still waits for its thread; reads, probes and observations pass `abandon_on_cancel=True` and keep their slot until the thread returns. Readiness bounds each probe by `PROBE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` and kills its CLI (`guest_probe.ProbeChild`) on cancellation or a stop `halted` reports mid-probe. The Docker events reader and native turns (`native_capacity()`) stay off the lane.
- Canonical names use the full agent UUID. Stop/remove only inspected container IDs with an exact `maraclaw.agent_id` ownership label; legacy short names are eligible only with that label. A conflicting recorded/canonical reference must fail before destructive work.
- Ordinary `start_container` delegates to `guest_start.py`: reuse a single owned running guest unchanged, resume an owned stopped guest in place, create only when absent, and require bounded application readiness before publishing `running`. Explicit replacement is separate (`rehydrate=False` for upgrades). Rehearse replacement config/native-file generation before admission and ownership checks; stop old owned writers before materializing storage or applying filesystem/config/auth writes. Those writes are not transactional. Do not promise atomic replacement or rollback.
- `agent_start_store.py` commits start intent before Docker work and conditionally publishes runtime against operation/owner/tenant/expected-container and fresh eligibility. Required worker loop `agent_start_reconciler.py` replays pending starts and cleans cancelled candidates; it must not restart arbitrary agents or defeat stop/disable/expiry/transfer/delete. Failed starts retain recoverable intent; failed stop commits `stopped` under the flock before 502. Preserve the explicit no-Docker `idle` compatibility path.
- Lock order is per-agent flock, then optional shared agents-root `_guest_admission` flock through admission and creation. Aggregate admission coordinates only cooperating engine starts on one host with the shared filesystem; it does not cover Docker restarts, manual Docker or multiple hosts.
- Guest limits come only from `guest_limits.resolve_guest_limits`: an ordinary agent in an organization gets its tier's memory (and equal swap), CPUs, CPU shares and PIDs (`organization_plan.py` holds the numbers); system agents and agents with no organization use the global `OPENCLAW_GUEST_*` settings with no CPU shares. They apply at creation and when `guest_start.py` resumes an `exited`/`created` guest: aggregate admission reserves the target memory first, then `docker update` (memory and swap together, CPUs, PIDs, CPU shares: the tier's, else Docker's default 1024) runs before `container.start`. A running guest is never updated. `OPENCLAW_GUEST_AGGREGATE_MEMORY_LIMIT_BYTES` must be 0 or at least `max(default, 5 GiB)`. Port allocation prefers the persisted in-range port and permits at most eight total attempts, retrying only recognized bind conflicts.
- `agent_stops.py` must read fresh stopped state under the per-agent lock and use compare-and-set for missing references. Preserve `agent_orphans.py` label/network/age checks and fresh locked ownership/upgrade guards for existing-agent duplicates. Neither path is a worker auto-restart feature. The stopped-container worker (`agent_stops.reconcile_stopped_containers`) runs as the heartbeat loop's single-flight child task alongside ticks, never awaited by one and never beating.
- Setup recovery (`agent_setup.py`, `GET /api/agents/{id}/setup`, `POST .../retry`) is manage-only with fresh principal/tenant checks and post-flock reauthorization. Keep its router free of request-wide CRUD binding. Retry accepts eligible failed work only, never steals running claims; successful replay clears durable credentials/files, returns `idle` and, for an eligible agent, queues the first start in the same transaction (`agent_start_dao.enqueue_if_eligible`).
- Deletion snapshots frozen task history and paths into `agent_deletion_cleanup` transactionally with row deletion. No FKs intentionally; cleanup survives agent/user/tenant removal. Post-commit callbacks accelerate, while the stopped-container worker durably retries. `agent_deletion_cleanup.py` removes owned writers before `agent_deletion_archive.py` publishes one resumable archive; Docker/ownership/archive failures retain workspace and pending intent. Baseline DDL is fresh-only, not an upgrade patch.
- Diagnostics are additive, safe observations of Docker health/OOM/limits/ownership/port drift, not readiness guarantees or automatic repair. `GET /api/agents/{id}/metrics` also returns `expected_limits` and `limits_drift` (inspected hard limits against `resolve_guest_limits`; both null when the target cannot be resolved).
- Workspace disk: `agent_workspace_quota.py` is the only policy (tier `OrganizationWorkspaceDiskBytes`; measured host agent directory plus container writable layer; soft, enforced at measurement time). The `agent_workspace_quota` worker loop (`agent_workspace_quota_worker.py`, every `AGENT_WORKSPACE_QUOTA_INTERVAL_SECONDS`, measuring up to `AGENT_WORKSPACE_QUOTA_CONCURRENCY` agents of a page at once and first the agents whose row asks for it) stores `agent_workspace_usage` and sends one creator `system_alert` per over-quota stretch. A walk past its deadline is recorded as `walk_failed_at` (`record_walk_failure`) and changes no size or block. `ensure_workspace_within_quota` runs right before `charge_agent_daily_message` in `chat_submissions`, `channels/submissions` and `channels/personal_burst_admission` only, so only new human work is refused (web `turn_rejected`/`quota_exceeded`, Personal `quota_rejected`, REST 507 `workspace_disk_quota_exceeded`); triggers, accepted work and report delivery are not. `files.py` delete calls `refresh_workspace_usage_after_delete`, which (like `remeasure_after_recreation`) waits at most `REQUEST_WAIT_SECONDS` and otherwise marks the row `remeasure_requested_at` for the worker. When the host files alone fit (`layer_caused_block`), messages say to stop and start the agent, and `guest_start.py` recreates a stopped owned guest on its own image through `replace_container` and runs `remeasure_after_recreation` instead of resuming it; files over the allowance say to delete files and resume as before, and a running guest is never recreated. The lifecycle projection reports `workspace_disk_exceeded`. Managed S3 quota stays in `storage_runtime/`.
- Resource pressure: `agent_resource_pressure.py` (episode state machine and recording), `agent_resource_pressure_docker.py` (batched CLI inspect and stats), `agent_resource_pressure_worker.py` (the `agent_resource_pressure` loop, every `AGENT_RESOURCE_PRESSURE_INTERVAL_SECONDS`), `agent_resource_pressure_events.py` (the long-lived `docker events` OOM reader the loop owns as a child task: anyio child shielded on spawn, terminated and reaped on cancellation, capped-backoff restart, never beats or raises; its module docstring covers the correlation with the per-pass `OOMKilled` check) and `agent_resource_pressure_report.py` (platform-admin summary, `suggested_tier`, keyset pages, CSV; served by `app/api/admin_resource_pressure.py`). Episodes in `agent_resource_pressure_episodes` cover non-system agents of organizations only and have no agent foreign key; workspace disk samples come from the quota pass through `record_workspace_disk_pressure`, which never raises. Sampling is periodic, so durations are lower bounds. Contracts: `../../docs/guest-containers.md`, `../../docs/admin-apis.md`.
- Workspace mirror (S3 only; local storage is the guest directory, so it is idle there and when `WORKSPACE_MIRROR_ENABLED=false`): `workspace_mirror.py` (the pass, the locks, `mirror_agent` for the worker, `sync_path` for share links), `workspace_mirror_plan.py` (pure decisions), `workspace_mirror_ops.py` (per-path operations), `workspace_mirror_local.py` (generation marker, contained probes), `workspace_mirror_scope.py` (the one `in_scope` predicate, the contained walk, guest-path normalization) and `workspace_mirror_worker.py` (the `workspace_mirror` loop, every `WORKSPACE_MIRROR_INTERVAL_SECONDS`). A start that rehydrates pulls first through `agent_materialize.pull_durable_state` under the exclusive setup lock. Manifest: `agent_workspace_mirror_entries` via `dao/workspace_mirror_entry_dao.py`. Lock order is the shared setup lock (`guest_write_section`), then the per-agent mirror flock, never the reverse; `reconcile_agent` takes no locks itself. Guest-directory access never follows a symlink (directory descriptors and the contained helpers in `storage_runtime/channel_files.py`). Contract: `../../docs/workspace-mirror.md`.
- File share links: `file_share_links.py` (`create_link`, `open_share`), `file_share_tokens.py` (Redis grants: TTL only, keyed by the SHA-256 of the token, the raw token is never stored), `schemas/file_share_links.py`, `api/file_shares.py` (public `/f/{token}/{filename}`) and `api/gateway.py` `POST /file-links`. Every unusable token answers the same 404, including one whose agent fails the gateway key predicate or has transfer cleanup pending. The default skill `file-share-links` is read from `agent_template/skills/file-share-links/SKILL.md` through `TEMPLATE_SKILL_FOLDERS` in `skill_seeder.py`; `push_default_skills_to_existing_agents` isolates failures per agent and holds no database transaction while storage is read or written (plan read, storage sweep, then the record write), so `idle_in_transaction_session_timeout` cannot end it before `default_skills_sync_hash` is saved. The `default_skills_sync_hash` setting hashes the folder, path and content of every default skill file, so a content edit reaches an existing agent's copy that is missing or still equals the previous sync; a copy its owner edited is kept. Contract: `../../docs/workspace-mirror.md`.

## Startup And Seeders

- Seeders are startup-path code and must be idempotent. `app.runtime.lifecycle.lifespan` can invoke `app.runtime.bootstrap.bootstrap` repeatedly when bootstrap is enabled; see `../runtime/AGENTS.md` for ownership.
- Keep optional seed/bootstrap failures scoped and logged; do not make optional seeders bring down unrelated roles unless the caller explicitly requires that.
- **Required system orgs:** `system_org_seeder.ensure_system_orgs()` seeds MaraClaw and KABERs in one transaction on fresh databases; each is created with its tier (MaraClaw Ultra, KABERs Pro, `SYSTEM_ORG_TIERS`), and every run sets `tenants.tier` back to it under the tenant row lock, writing only on a difference. A missing KABERs genesis org admin requires `KABERS_ORG_ADMIN_EMAIL` / `KABERS_ORG_ADMIN_PASSWORD`; an existing genesis is unchanged. New genesis must change password on first login, and bootstrap does not claim its email domain. All system-org seed failures propagate. Replicas booting together serialize on a transaction-level advisory lock (`maraclaw:seed:system-orgs`) taken first in that transaction, so the second reads the committed organizations instead of failing on a unique index.
- **Exception - genesis platform admin:** `platform_admin_seeder.ensure_platform_admin()` is **required** on bootstrap. It first requires usable genesis credentials in the database; only then falls back to `PLATFORM_ADMIN_EMAIL` / `PLATFORM_ADMIN_PASSWORD`. Raises `PlatformAdminSeedError` when neither source is present. `app.runtime.bootstrap` propagates every PA seed failure (fail-closed). Do not demote this to warn-only. It reads the genesis once without a lock (the usual rerun); only when it must seed does it take the advisory lock `maraclaw:seed:platform-admin` and read again inside the creating transaction, so two replicas on an empty database leave one genesis admin and both return.
- Platform admin rules: create path sets `must_change_password=True`; existing identity only elevates if env password verifies (never elevate by email alone; never re-enable a disabled identity; never overwrite password when genesis credentials already exist); genesis membership is the **MaraClaw** system org.
- Agent seeders (`agent_seeder`) look up `first_by_role("platform_admin")` and run **after** platform admin seed.
- New companies: `tenant_provisioning.create_tenant_with_org_admin` (required `tier` and `phone`, written with the tenant; slug + identity + `org_admin` + participant + `bind_org_member`). Used by `POST /api/tenants/` and `POST /api/admin/companies` (the body's tier) and by the admission approval (the request's tier, which it also records on its own request row as history, and the request's phone). Duplicate admin email → `AdminEmailTakenError`. The genesis admin email host is claimed as the tenant's default email domain (`techadmin@marathon.vn` → `marathon.vn`). Domain already claimed → `DomainClaimedError`.
- Disable/enable a company: `tenant_lifecycle.set_tenant_active`. MaraClaw/KABERs (`is_system` / `is_default_end_user_org`) cannot be disabled. Disable deactivates members (not `platform_admin`), stops agents, and turns off triggers/schedules. Enable restores members only (agents/automations stay stopped).
- Reset/verify email links: `frontend_origin.resolve_frontend_base_url`.
- Additional admins: `admin_provisioning.py`. Genesis is `users.is_genesis`. Only those callers may create another of the same role, or activate/deactivate the other admins of that role.
- Admin trail: `admin_audit.py` → `admin_audit_logs` (actor, action, time, `changes` before/after). Distinct from agent-scoped `audit_logs`. Never raise on write failure.
- New default tools/templates/agents should preserve tenant/global visibility assumptions already encoded in the current seeders.

## Connectors

- Long-running connector runtimes use manager singletons such as `feishu_ws_manager`, `dingtalk_stream_manager`, `wecom_stream_manager`, `wechat_poll_manager`, and `discord_gateway_manager`.
- Connector managers run only under the `connector` role: lifespan opens them **after** `init_pool` and `runtime/connectors.py` drives each `reconcile_clients`. Do not start process-wide loops from ordinary request handlers; after saving or deleting the row a handler may call one `start_client`/`stop_client`, in a connector-role process only.
- Preserve provider-specific identity semantics such as Feishu `user_id` vs `open_id`, WeCom `external_id` vs `unionid`, and SSO secret differences.
- Slack and Teams are webhook/API integrations, not lifespan daemon managers.
- Personal WhatsApp and Zalo (QR) use connector-role supervisors, not manager singletons: `channels/whatsapp_personal_supervisor.py` and `channels/zalo_personal_supervisor.py` own the Node bridges in `connectors/whatsapp` and `connectors/zalo`, respectively. The required worker loop `openclaw_dispatch_reconciler.py` recovers pending native lane heads, including due Personal bursts, in keyset pages of 50 with a one-second scan interval; it never replays delivered work. Exact guest evidence can settle an unconsumed interruption (`openclaw_interrupted_inputs.py`) or adopt an already published successful recovery receipt (`openclaw_recovered_inputs.py`, `openclaw_recovered_dispatch.py`). Contract: `channels/AGENTS.md` and `../../docs/shared-openclaw-runtime.md`.
- `company_transfer_cleanup.reconcile_transfer_cleanup()` returns a bool: `False` only when the pending queue could not be read. The connector-role `transfer_cleanup` loop beats only on `True`; the stopped-container worker and transfer-disposition callers ignore the result. Every IM manager's `<provider>-reconcile` loop survives `reconcile_clients` errors with capped backoff (`runtime/AGENTS.md`).
- Auth providers, SSO, Google OAuth and IM channels remain supported independently of retired provider directory sync. Do not remove login, identity binding or channel behavior when changing shared organization members.
- Native dispatch schedules one owned task per conversation lane, bounded by `OPENCLAW_AGENT_MAX_PARALLEL_TURNS`; each human lane (a user's canonical conversation) is FIFO and different users' lanes run in parallel. `openclaw_gateway_stream.py` owns bounded best-effort web deltas, `openclaw_gateway_delivery.py` owns terminal persistence/publication, `response_identity.py` owns the identity prompt and configured-model-id redaction applied at both, and `gateway_report_wake.py` accelerates the durable relay after commit. Wire correlation is `turn_id`, distinct from the assistant `message_id`. See `../../docs/shared-openclaw-runtime.md` and `../../docs/gateway-report-delivery.md` before changing these boundaries.
